Open GitHub repository ↗

This downloads verified-gearing.json with your proposed change. Review it, replace the repository’s verified-gearing.json on the GitHub Pages publishing branch, and commit. Only people with repository write access can publish. A download does not verify the model; it becomes globally verified after GitHub Pages publishes your commit. Online users receive the update when they reopen the app; offline users keep their last catalogue.
