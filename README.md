## Features

- **Electric:** Estimate rotor RPM from motor KV, battery cells/voltage, gearing, throttle and efficiency.
- **Nitro:** Calculate head speed using estimated or measured engine RPM, with 2-, 3- and 4-gear drivetrain options.
- **Model database:** Select helicopters and source-backed verified gearing, or enter your own setup.
- **Manage Models:** Review unverified models, save custom setups, and export/import backups. Shared verified gearing is published through GitHub.
- **Guided tutorial:** Step-by-step help for both power types.
- **Installable PWA:** Mobile/desktop use with offline caching after initial load.

## Getting Started

1. Open the [calculator](https://harrykd33.github.io/KevinD-RC-RPM-Calculator/).
2. Choose **Electric (E)** or **Nitro (N)**.
3. Select a helicopter or custom gearing, enter your setup, and review estimated head speed.

**Note:** Results are estimates, not measured RPM. Verify gearing and actual head speed before flying. Custom models stay in your browser unless exported; they do not sync automatically.
